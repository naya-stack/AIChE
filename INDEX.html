The Problem (5nm Scale)

  • Semiconductor walls become atomically thin.
  • Electrons penetrate via Quantum Tunneling.
  • Leakage current, Heat, Battery Drain.

The Solution: High-κ (HfO₂)

  • Hafnium-based materials (HfO₂, HfSiON).
  • Physically thicker layer, same capacitance.
  • Suppresses tunneling exponentially.

Tunneling Probability ($T$):

\[ T \propto e^{-2\kappa d} \]

Thicker barrier ($d$) = Massive drop in leakage

Why High-κ Materials Actually Work

The Barrier Height

In SiO₂, the barrier is thin. Electron wavefunctions (probability clouds) extend completely across the dielectric.

HfO₂ Solution: The atomic structure of Hafnium Oxide creates a larger effective band gap.

  • Higher barrier height ($\Phi_B$)
  • Faster decay of wavefunction
  • Reduced defect-mediated transport
